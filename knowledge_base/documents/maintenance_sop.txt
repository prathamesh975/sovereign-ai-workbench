MAINTENANCE SOP
Cooling Water Pump Maintenance

Document ID: SOP-MNT-001
Revision: 3
Department: Maintenance

1. PURPOSE

This SOP defines the procedure for inspection and corrective
maintenance of cooling water pumps.

2. MECHANICAL SEAL LEAKAGE

Minor leakage around the mechanical seal shall be recorded
during inspection.

If leakage is confirmed, the mechanical seal should be
inspected and replaced when required.

3. VIBRATION

Pump vibration shall be monitored during operation.

A vibration reading above the department's acceptable
operating range requires further investigation.

The maintenance team shall record the vibration measurement
and inspect possible causes including coupling alignment,
bearing condition and mechanical looseness.

4. COUPLING ALIGNMENT

If coupling misalignment is identified, corrective alignment
shall be performed before returning the equipment to normal
operation.

5. POST-MAINTENANCE INSPECTION

After corrective maintenance:

- Verify that the mechanical seal is functioning correctly.
- Recheck pump vibration.
- Verify coupling alignment.
- Record the maintenance action.
- Continue monitoring the equipment.

6. DOCUMENTATION

Inspection findings and corrective actions must be documented
in the maintenance record.
