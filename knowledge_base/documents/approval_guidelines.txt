APPROVAL NOTE GUIDELINES

Document ID: ENG-APP-002
Department: Engineering

Approval notes for maintenance activities should contain:

1. Subject
2. Equipment identification
3. Inspection reference
4. Summary of inspection findings
5. Recommended corrective actions
6. Operational considerations
7. Requested approval
8. Supporting documents

The approval note should clearly distinguish observed findings
from recommended actions.

Technical measurements from the inspection report should be
retained accurately.

Where maintenance is recommended, the approval note should
identify the required corrective action and any recommended
follow-up inspection.
